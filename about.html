Great pet care shouldn't require a yard — or a guessing game.

Healthy House Pets is built for people raising dogs and cats in apartments, condos, and other small spaces, where a lot of mainstream pet advice — big yards, dedicated mudrooms, acreage to burn off energy — just doesn't apply. We test and write about what actually works in a one-bedroom: fresh and subscription dog food, training that keeps the neighbors happy, and smart pet tech that earns its counter space.

How we choose what to cover

We focus on three areas where small-space pet owners have the most questions and the fewest good answers:

  • Fresh & subscription dog food — portioning, cost per month, and how brands hold up over a full feeding trial, not just a first impression.
  • Training that works in tight quarters — noise control, leash manners on short walks, and methods that don't assume you have a yard to practice in.
  • Smart pet tech & odor control — how self-cleaning litter boxes and odor systems perform in a closed apartment, not an open showroom.
